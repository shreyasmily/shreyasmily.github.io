ENSO SST Analysis

EOF (Empirical Orthogonal Function) analysis of El Niño–Southern Oscillation variability in tropical Pacific sea surface temperature, using NOAA's gridded OISST satellite record (2016–2024). EOF1 recovers the classic ENSO pattern and explains 31.3% of SST anomaly variance; thresholding its principal component identifies individual El Niño / La Niña events, including the 2020–22 "triple-dip" La Niña.

India Rainfall Variability

A companion project studying the Indian summer monsoon: the All-India Rainfall Index (AIRI) and regional rainfall measures, their relationship to ENSO and the Indian Ocean Dipole, and EOF-based spatial patterns of monsoon variability — built on a 120-year gridded rainfall record and SST data.
